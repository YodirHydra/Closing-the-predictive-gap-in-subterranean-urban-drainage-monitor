# Supplementary Note 1 | Literature search and study selection

**Information sources.** The records available for this synthesis do not document the databases searched, search strings, Boolean operators, filters or search dates. For the 50 records S24-S73, the matrix records the publisher or host of each record: IEEE (18, including one Scopus-indexed IEEE record), ScienceDirect/Elsevier (8), Springer (7), MDPI (3), IWA Publishing (2), Wiley/Hindawi (2), and one record each from arXiv, IOS Press, IOP Publishing, IET, EDP Sciences, NepJOL, Taylor's University, Zenodo, NOJEST and Nanotechnology Perceptions. This field identifies where each retained record is hosted; it does not identify which databases were searched. No equivalent field exists for S1-S23. One record (S25) is an arXiv preprint of a conference paper.

**Eligibility criteria.** No inclusion or exclusion criteria were documented. The extraction fields common to both blocks of records were wireless connection, gas sensor, map for user, ML/AI, research gap, country, year and alert mechanism. The following criteria are inferred from the retained records and were not pre-specified: (i) topic, namely drainage, sewer, manhole, stormwater or waterlogging/flood monitoring or prediction; (ii) technology, namely sensor-based monitoring (water level, flow, gas, cover status) with wireless or cloud communication (GSM/GPRS, Wi-Fi, ZigBee, LoRa/LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, LTE-M, Sigfox), ML/AI applied to drainage, sewer or flood problems, or reviews of IoT or ML in water and drainage systems; (iii) publication years 2013-2026 (observed range); (iv) English-language titles; and (v) publication types observed (conference papers, journal articles, book chapters, reviews and one preprint). Exclusion criteria cannot be reconstructed because no excluded record is available; only the complement of the criteria above could be assumed. The retained set is heterogeneous: it includes manhole-cover monitoring (S28, S38, S39), image-based sewer defect detection (S40, S41, S50), process-model sulfide prediction (S48, S49) and flood forecasting that is not sewer-specific (S71, S72), which were retained as adjacent literature.

**Study selection and verification depth.** Seventy-three records were retained: 23 in the original matrix (S1-S23) and 50 added records (S24-S73). A title-level comparison found no identical titles between the two blocks; DOI-level de-duplication was not possible because S1-S23 carry no DOI. The matrix does not state whether the two blocks arose from separate searches or screening stages, and we do not claim that all 73 records were independently assessed or read in full text. For S24-S73, the verification note records the depth of reading used for data extraction: full text for 4 records (S25, S59, S65, S68), partial text for 2 (S69, S73), abstract or keyword level for 40, a review record with no stated depth for 2 (S56, S61), and a data-quality note only for 2 (S46, S47: inferred country and truncated title). This describes data verification, not eligibility assessment. The depth of reading for S1-S23 is not recorded.
